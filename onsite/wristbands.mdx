The **Wristbands** workspace is for Tap events only. Open it from the [Command Center](/onsite/command-center).

<Warning>
Balances belong to people, not bands. Replacing a band doesn't move money, and freezing a band doesn't freeze the balance. To stop spending, freeze the wallet from the participant's profile. See [Participants](/onsite/participants).
</Warning>

## Wristband list

Search by wristband ID, Ticket ID or holder email. **Not working** lists stopped bands.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/onsite/onsite-wristband-list.svg" alt="Wristbands list with search, state column and row actions" />
</Frame>

Row actions: **Freeze** / **Unfreeze** (band stops or resumes working for entry and payment), **Take off** (ends the binding) and **Bind to ticket** (asks for the Ticket ID). They use the [action panel](/onsite/tickets#the-action-panel). Freezing leaves the balance and ticket unchanged; taking off leaves the balance, entry history and purchases untouched.

**Bind to ticket** follows the event's setting: **Put a wristband on and check in**, or **Put a wristband on** (the person enters when they tap at the gate).

## Add wristbands

Select **+ Add wristbands** to register spare bands before doors open.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/onsite/onsite-wristband-add-panel.svg" alt="Add wristbands panel with three UIDs and Add 3 bands button" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the bands">
    One UID or printed ID per line. A scanner that sends a new line per tap fills the box for you. Formatting doesn't matter, and duplicates are ignored.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a note (optional)">
    For example, which box the bands came from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Add">
    Maximum 200 at a time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

This creates no wallet and binds nothing. The result lists each band as newly registered, already available, or refused with a reason. Check each line: a band shown as newly registered when you expected an existing one usually means a mistyped ID.

## Wristband page

Select a band to see its summary, **Band history** and **Bindings**. Refused attempts and replaced bindings are kept on purpose, so "what happened" and "which band did they have then" can always be answered. **Add note** opens the [case form](/onsite/cases).

</Frame>

### Available actions

Each action says what it does and what it leaves alone; one that can't be used says why.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/onsite/onsite-wristband-available-actions.svg" alt="Available actions panel with a restricted action and an unavailable action" />
</Frame>

| Action | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Put a wristband on** | Binds the band to a ticket. No balance moves. |
| **Replace the wristband** | Retires the old band and binds a new one (enter its ID) to the same ticket. |
| **Take the wristband off** | Ends the binding. Doesn't check them out or touch the balance. |
| **Report the wristband lost or stolen** | Stops the band and ends any binding. No replacement issued; wallet not frozen. |
| **Freeze** / **Unfreeze the wristband** | Stops or restores this band only. The balance stays spendable on any other band they hold. |
| **Put a wristband on anyway** (Restricted) | Binds past a failed check. Doesn't fix what was objected to. |
| **Force rebind the wristband** (Restricted) | Moves the band off the current holder's ticket to another. Their entry history and purchases stay. |

Most ask for a reason. **Restricted** actions are supervisor-only, are recorded against your name, and need at least 12 characters in **What did you verify?** plus the confirmation tick box.

### Ownership conflicts

If a ticket tried to bind a band already bound to someone else, the page shows **Binding blocked** and an **Ownership conflict** panel comparing **Currently bound** with **Requesting bind**. Whoever has entry and spending on the band has been using it.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/onsite/onsite-wristband-ownership-conflict.svg" alt="Binding blocked label and Ownership conflict panel" />
</Frame>

Rebinding detaches both people from the band they wear, so settle ownership first. Select **Investigate ownership** to record the case and escalate it. A supervisor can then use **Force rebind the wristband**.
