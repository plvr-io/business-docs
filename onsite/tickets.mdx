title: "Tickets & Entry"
description: "Review tickets, see why someone can't get in, and resolve entry problems."

**Tickets & Entry** shows whether a ticket is valid, why someone can't get in, and how to fix it. Open it from the [Command Center](/onsite/command-center), or select **Resolve** on a search result.

<Note>
For ticket types, sales and comp tickets, see [Ticket management](/tickets/ticket-management).
</Note>

## The ticket list

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/onsite/onsite-tickets-list.svg" alt="Tickets and Entry page with summary cards, view buttons, ticket table and Why blocked panel" />
</Frame>

The view buttons (Needs attention is the default) and **Filter** narrow the list by state, Ticket ID, holder name or email. **Awaiting details** tickets can be recovered at the desk: take the details, then admit with an override.

The **Why blocked** panel is your work queue. Each reason shows a count and next step, and is either:

- **Recoverable at the desk**: fix it or use an override. Listed first, so work them in that order.
- **Not recoverable here**: no desk action will fix it. Record a [service case](/onsite/cases).

Selecting a reason filters the list to those tickets.

**Recent entry activity** at the bottom logs desk actions. **Done** succeeded, **Blocked** was refused, **Failed** did not complete.

  <img src="/images/onsite/onsite-tickets-recent-entry-activity.svg" alt="Recent entry activity table with Done, Blocked and Failed results" />
</Frame>

## Resolve one ticket

Select **Resolve** on a ticket. The page title states the situation, and the verdict is **Ready**, **Needs a look** or **Blocked**. A ticket can be Valid and still need a look.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/onsite/onsite-ticket-resolution-page.svg" alt="Guided resolution page with verdict, step bar, validation summary and say to the participant box" />
</Frame>

The **Validation summary** checks ticket status, session, attendee details and entry history. A box beneath says what is wrong, what to do next, and a sentence to read to the participant. **See everything for this person** opens the [participant profile](/onsite/participants).

On a Tap event, the event's setting decides whether binding a wristband also checks the person in (**Bind wristband & check in**; if either half fails, neither applies) or entry waits for a tap at the gate (**Bind wristband only**). You don't choose.

## What you can do

The first recommended action leads in a highlighted box. **Show everything else** expands the rest. Actions that can't exist for the event aren't shown; a refused action explains why, and the attempt is recorded. **Record a service case** is there when nothing works.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/onsite/onsite-ticket-what-you-can-do.svg" alt="What you can do panel beside the confirmation preview" />
</Frame>

| Action | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Check in** / **Check in manually** | Ticket-only events. Marks the ticket entered; manual is recorded as such. |
| **Admit with an override** | Admits past a block clearable at the desk. The block stays visible on the ticket. |
| **Allow re-entry** | Records a return as its own event; the first check-in is never overwritten. |
| **Undo check-in** | Returns the ticket to unused. Doesn't reverse later purchases or remove the entry from the log. |

Tap events admit by wristband tap, so recovery goes through the wristband. See [Wristbands](/onsite/wristbands), and [Participants](/onsite/participants) and [Cashless](/onsite/cashless) for wallet actions. Before an undo, **If you undo this entry** lists anything that followed it.

## The action panel

Every action opens the same side panel.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/onsite/onsite-action-panel.svg" alt="Admit with an override action panel with Confirm disabled" />
</Frame>

Check **You are acting on** (1) before anything else. Pick a **Reason** (3); a note alone isn't enough. **Confirm** stays disabled until everything required is filled in. If an action fails, nothing is changed and you can retry or record a case.

<Warning>
Overrides need at least 12 characters in **What did you verify?** (who approved it, what you checked, how you confirmed who they are) plus the tick box. Your name is recorded.
</Warning>

## Permissions

Actions marked **Supervisor** are shown to everyone but only a supervisor can use them. Otherwise ask one, or open a case.

<Note>
The console does not issue refunds or void tickets. Record them as [service cases](/onsite/cases) for review.
</Note>
