A participant profile (**Participant 360**) puts one person's identity, tickets, entry and cashless activity on one screen. Reach it by selecting a person in [Search](/onsite/search), or from a link on a ticket, wristband, transaction or case. **+ Create service case** records a problem.

<Note>
A person can hold several tickets and wristbands on a multi-session event, but shares one wallet. The balance never lives on a band.
</Note>

## Overview

The **Overview** tab opens with a verdict: **Ready** (go ahead), **Needs a look** (read the notes first) or **Blocked** (resolve something first).

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/onsite/onsite-participant-overview.svg" alt="Participant profile Overview tab with verdict, what needs attention, recommended action and tabs" />
</Frame>

- **What needs attention** says what is wrong, the next step, and a "Say" sentence to read to the participant. If it can't be fixed at the desk, use **Record a service case**.
- **Recommended action** with **Start resolution** opens the ticket's guided resolution. See [Tickets & Entry](/onsite/tickets).
- **Bought for other people** lists tickets this person purchased for others. They hold none of them, so don't admit them on one; use **Open that person**.

## Tabs

The **Tickets** tab has **Resolve** per ticket. **Wristbands** and **Cashless** appear on Tap events; replaced bands stay listed. **Activity** is a timeline of what has happened for this person.

### Cashless

The tab shows the wallet balance, whether it is Active or Frozen, and recent money. For the event-wide ledger, see [Cashless](/onsite/cashless).

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/onsite/onsite-participant-cashless-tab.svg" alt="Participant Cashless tab with wallet balance, action buttons and recent money" />
</Frame>

| Action | Use it to | Not for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Correct the balance** | Fix a misrecorded amount. | Refunds or top-ups. It creates or reverses no payment. |
| **Issue a reward credit** | Add goodwill money, with a label the participant sees. | Corrections. |
| **Freeze the wallet** / **Unfreeze the wallet** | Stop or restore all cashless spending on every band they hold. | Entry, or freezing a band. |

These use the [action panel](/onsite/tickets#the-action-panel). **Correct the balance** also needs:

- the amount the balance is **out by** (not the total it should be), in whole currency, with a minus to take money off. **Will apply** shows the signed amount before you confirm;
- **Evidence**, such as a cash count, terminal slip or receipt number;
- **What did you verify?** (at least 12 characters) and the confirmation tick box.

**Issue a reward credit** needs a positive amount in whole currency and a label.

## What you can do

The **What you can do** panel offers every action for the selected ticket, wristband and wallet, recommended first, the rest under **Show everything else**. See [Tickets & Entry](/onsite/tickets), [Wristbands](/onsite/wristbands) and the wallet actions above.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/onsite/onsite-participant-what-you-can-do.svg" alt="What you can do panel with recommended action, everything else expanded and a supervisor-only action" />
</Frame>

- With several tickets, actions apply to the ticket you selected in search, otherwise the first one. Check **You are acting on** before confirming.
- **Supervisor** actions are disabled unless you are a supervisor.
- If nothing resolves the problem, use **Record a service case**. An open case also shows a **Service context** panel; see [Service cases](/onsite/cases).
