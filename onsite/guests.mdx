---

The **Guests** workspace is for invite lists. Open it from the [Command Center](/onsite/command-center). Search by name or email; the list shows 25 per page.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/onsite/onsite-guests-list.svg" alt="Guests list with search, duplicate warning and check-in buttons" />
</Frame>

<Warning>
A name is not an identity. If several guests could match, a warning shows under the name. Confirm who the person is before admitting them.
</Warning>

## Admit a guest

Select **Check in** on the guest's row. No reason is needed.

## Undo a check-in

Select **Undo check-in**, choose a **Reason** (required), optionally add a **Note**, and select **Confirm**. The guest-list entry can be used again, but the original admission stays in the activity log.

## Add an approved guest

Select **Add an approved guest** for someone who wasn't on the list.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/onsite/onsite-guests-add-approved-form.svg" alt="Add an approved guest form" />
</Frame>

All three fields are required:

- **Full name:** as they'll give it at the door.
- **Ticket type ID:** the ticket type the guest counts against. Find it in [Ticket management](/tickets/ticket-management).
- **Who approved this?** Who and when, for example "Approved by the promoter by phone, 20:15". Without it nobody can later tell whether the addition was authorised.
