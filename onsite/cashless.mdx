The **Cashless** workspace shows where money was collected, whether the balance was credited, and where it was spent. It is available on Tap events with cashless enabled. Open it from the [Command Center](/onsite/command-center).

<Note>
For event-wide reporting see [Cashless activity](/tap/cashless-activity). To configure top-ups see [Top-up setup](/tap/top-up-setup).
</Note>

Everything is split by channel. **Online** top-ups went through a payment provider and have a payment reference. **Onsite** ones were booked at a station against cash or a terminal and have none; reconcile them by station, method and cash record.

## Unresolved top-ups

The bar at the top counts top-ups where money may have moved without the balance following (pending, expired or failed). **Show N** filters the ledger to them. An expired top-up is not treated as free: it stays flagged until someone checks.

If any wallets are frozen, a notice says how many. A frozen wallet stops spending, not entry; freezing the wristband is separate.

## Money ledger

Every movement of money is a row with its amount and the balance it left behind. Search by email, name, reference, station or stall, and filter by channel, type or **Unresolved only**. Select a row's arrow for its [transaction detail](#transaction-detail).

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/onsite/onsite-cashless-ledger.svg" alt="Money ledger with filters and an Investigate link" />
</Frame>

The **Top-up stations** panel shows each station's activity with a health label (Healthy, Watch, Degraded).

## What the console cannot do

The only money write is **Correct the balance**, done from the wallet. See [Participants](/onsite/participants#cashless). Crediting an unconfirmed top-up, moving one between wallets, and refunding a balance or vendor charge need a payment-provider check or a policy decision, so record each as a [service case](/onsite/cases).

## Top-up detail

Select **Investigate** on an unresolved top-up to open **Top-up not reflected**. It shows three layers that must agree: **Payment provider**, **Top-up entry** and **Wallet balance**. **Where it broke** gives the diagnosis.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/onsite/onsite-cashless-topup-investigation.svg" alt="Top-up not reflected page with three layers, Reconciliation and Where it broke" />
</Frame>

<Warning>
Expired does not mean not charged. Don't tell a guest no money was taken unless you have checked.
</Warning>

- If PLVR shows the provider took the money but the balance never rose, the guest is owed the credit. Don't ask them to pay again.
- If the top-up expired but the guest can show a charge on a statement, believe them and open a payment review (**Open a payment review case**).
- A second credit can't be issued while the original payment is unresolved.
- If a reference matches nothing, check for 0/O and 1/I mix-ups. With a receipt, record an unmatched case with the reference exactly as given.

## Transaction detail

The top band shows status, type, amount, balance after and now, the participant, and a sentence you can read aloud to them. Below it, **What was bought** lists items and refunds so far, and **What you can do** suggests next steps.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/onsite/onsite-cashless-transaction-detail.svg" alt="Transaction detail with a partly refunded purchase" />
</Frame>

Check the refunded column before agreeing to anything further. "Not on this event" means the reference belongs to another event or is mistyped.
