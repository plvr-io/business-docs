title: "Service cases"
description: "Record desk problems, attach evidence and conclude each with a clear answer."
---

A service case records a problem raised at the desk and how it ended, so nobody is turned away with nothing recorded. Nothing is assigned or timed. Open the list from **Service cases** in the [Command Center](/onsite/command-center).

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/onsite/onsite-cases-list.svg" alt="Service cases list with filter buttons and case table" />
</Frame>

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Open** | Still being worked on. |
| **Sent for review** | The desk couldn't safely act, so a reviewer has it. |
| **Resolved** | The participant got a definite answer. |
| **Could not be resolved** | Not possible, with a reason. Counted as a product gap. |
| **Cancelled** | Withdrawn, or a duplicate. |

Risk (High, Medium, Normal) is separate from status. Review types are Identity dispute, Payment review, Ticket entitlement review and Operational incident.

## Record a case

Select **Record a case**. The same form opens from tickets, transactions and zero-result searches, pre-filled where possible. The state of any attached ticket or transaction is saved with the case, so a reviewer sees what you saw.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/onsite/onsite-cases-record-form.svg" alt="Record a service case form" />
</Frame>

- If nobody is identified, enter what they showed you exactly as given. Don't correct spelling or capitalization.
- Describe what you checked, what you found and what you couldn't do.

## Work a case

The case page shows the status, where the case is in its flow, what the portal showed when it was raised, the evidence and every action tried. Refused attempts are kept on purpose: "we tried and it was refused" is evidence.

  <img src="/images/onsite/onsite-case-detail-page.svg" alt="Service case page with state flow, evidence, attempts and Conclude buttons" />
</Frame>

### Add evidence

Select **Add evidence** and choose **Reference number** (payment reference, order number, cash count), **Named confirmation** (what was confirmed and by whom) or **Note**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/onsite/onsite-case-add-evidence-dialog.svg" alt="Add evidence panel with Named confirmation" />
</Frame>

<Tip>
Verbal agreement isn't evidence. Record a reference number or who confirmed what.
</Tip>

### Link to a participant

For a case with nobody identified, find the person in [Search](/onsite/search), then select **Link to a participant** and enter their participant user ID. Linking doesn't change the status.

### Conclude

Under **Conclude this case**, you only see the moves available to you. Desk operators, for example, can't choose "Conclude: cannot be resolved".

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/onsite/onsite-case-conclude-buttons.svg" alt="Conclude this case buttons and Send for review with review type dropdown" />
</Frame>

| Button | Required |
| --- | --- |
| **Send for review** | The kind of review. |
| **Close as resolved** | The outcome: what they were told and what you did. |
| **Conclude: cannot be resolved** | Why, i.e. what the console couldn't do. |
| **Cancel this case** / **Reopen** | Nothing. |

Select **Confirm** to apply.
