Welcome to the PLVR Business Portal guide. The portal is where you manage your organization, publish events, sell tickets, run promotions, and operate your event on the day.

This guide is written for organizers, not engineers. It shows you what to set up, in what order, and who on your team should do it.

<Card title="New here?" icon="list-check" href="/getting-started/onboarding">
  Start with Onboarding: sign up, create your organization, complete your profile and add your team.
</Card>

Pick the view that fits how you think about your event.
