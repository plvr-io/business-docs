For regular events such as a monthly night or a series, where success depends on people returning.

## What to set up

1. **Keep your profile current.** [Organization settings](/organization/settings) is the constant between events.
2. **Understand your audience.** [Audience](/organization/audience) shows who attends and who returns, with an exportable list.
3. **Grow followers.** See [Followers](/events/followers).
4. **Re-engage by email.** [Email campaigns](/promotions/campaigns) to ticket holders, followers or your own list.
5. **Use RSVP if you approve guests.** See [RSVP](/events/rsvp).
6. **Reward loyalty.** [Promo codes](/promotions/promo-codes) for returning guests.
7. **Learn what works.** [Tracking links](/promotions/tracking-links) per channel.

Each new event is still created with the PLVR team. Email partners@plvr.io, and see [Ticketing concepts](/tickets/concepts) for what to ask for.

## Skip

- Multi-day structures and bundles, unless an edition needs them.
- PLVR Tap, unless you want cashless at an edition.

## Team

Usually a small [core team](/roles/core-team) plus volunteer gate crew. See also [Marketing](/roles/marketing-team) and [Data](/roles/data-team).

## Pitfalls

- Looking at one event's sales instead of [Audience](/organization/audience) across events.
- Emailing the same list too often.
