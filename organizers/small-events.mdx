For a workshop, party or meetup with one or two ticket types and a small team.

## What to set up

1. **Get your event created.** Not self-service yet: email partners@plvr.io and tell them what tickets you want to sell.
2. **Finish your profile and add your team.** [Onboarding](/getting-started/onboarding), [Organization settings](/organization/settings), [Team](/organization/team).
3. **Complete your event page.** [Event editor](/events/event-editor).
4. **Check your tickets.** [Ticket Management](/tickets/ticket-management). Optionally stage prices with [General Admission in tiers](/tickets/scenario-ga-tiers).
5. **Share the page.** One [tracking link](/promotions/tracking-links) per place you post it.
6. **On the day.** Create gate staff codes in [Onsite staff](/onsite/onsite-staff) and use the [Command Center](/onsite/command-center).

## Skip

- Bundles, add-ons and multiple sessions.
- Affiliates and email campaigns, until you have an audience.
- PLVR Tap, unless you want cashless.

## Team

Often one or two people cover everything. See the [roles overview](/roles/index), [Core team](/roles/core-team) and [Scanning team](/roles/scanning-team).

## Pitfalls

- Asking PLVR for your event or ticket changes at the last minute.
- Gate staff without login codes at doors open.
- Free guests should get [complimentary tickets](/tickets/scenario-comp-guests), not buy.
