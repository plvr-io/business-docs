For multi-day events with several ticket types, packages, vendors and a big onsite team.

## What to set up

1. **Plan with PLVR early.** Event creation, sessions (one per day), ticket types and bundles are arranged with your PLVR representative. Ask for PLVR Tap to be enabled if you want it.
2. **Understand your tickets.** [Ticketing concepts](/tickets/concepts), [Multi-day festival](/tickets/scenario-multi-day-festival), [Group packages with bundles](/tickets/scenario-bundles), [VIP tickets and add-ons](/tickets/scenario-vip-addons).
3. **Manage tiers and add-ons** in [Ticket Management](/tickets/ticket-management).
4. **Set up PLVR Tap.** [Overview](/tap/introduction), [Top-up setup](/tap/top-up-setup), [Vendors](/tap/vendors), [Approvals](/tap/approvals), [Personalization form](/tap/personalization-form).
5. **Promote.** [Promo codes](/promotions/promo-codes), [tracking links](/promotions/tracking-links), [affiliates](/promotions/affiliates), [email campaigns](/promotions/campaigns).
6. **Handle guests.** [Comping guests, artists and press](/tickets/scenario-comp-guests).
7. **Prepare onsite.** [Onsite staff](/onsite/onsite-staff), [Command Center](/onsite/command-center), [Wristbands](/onsite/wristbands), [Cashless](/onsite/cashless), [Cases](/onsite/cases).
8. **Afterwards.** [Spending report](/tap/spending-report), [Cashless activity](/tap/cashless-activity), [Ticket report](/tickets/ticket-report).

## Skip

- Duplicate tickets. Keep tiers consistent across days.
- Table booking, unless you take venue reservations.

## Team

Use every role: [Core](/roles/core-team), [Scanning](/roles/scanning-team), [Data](/roles/data-team), [Marketing](/roles/marketing-team). Give each gate, binding and top-up team its own login codes.

## Pitfalls

- Asking PLVR late for a new day or ticket type. You cannot add them yourself.
- Bundles draw on the same inventory as the underlying tickets.
- Vendor menus not approved before doors open.
