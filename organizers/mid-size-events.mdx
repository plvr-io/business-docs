For events with several ticket tiers, a marketing push through partners and channels, and a gate team on the day.

## What to set up

1. **Agree your ticket plan with PLVR.** Ticket types, sessions and bundles are arranged with your PLVR representative. Read [Ticketing concepts](/tickets/concepts) first.
2. **Set up tier sales.** Use a release chain so each tier opens after the last. See [General Admission in tiers](/tickets/scenario-ga-tiers) and [Ticket Management](/tickets/ticket-management).
3. **Prepare the event page.** [Event editor](/events/event-editor), [Registration form](/events/registration-form).
4. **Promote.**
   - [Promo codes](/promotions/promo-codes) for partners or limited offers ([Discount campaigns](/tickets/scenario-promo-discounts)).
   - [Tracking links](/promotions/tracking-links) per channel.
   - [Affiliates](/promotions/affiliates) for commission.
5. **Handle guests.** [Complimentary tickets](/tickets/scenario-comp-guests) for artists, press and sponsors.
6. **Prepare scanning.** [Onsite staff](/onsite/onsite-staff), [Command Center](/onsite/command-center).
7. **Watch sales.** [Ticket report](/tickets/ticket-report), [Audience](/organization/audience).

## Skip

- PLVR Tap, unless cashless is planned.
- Multiple sessions, unless the event runs over several days.

## Team

Split work across [Core](/roles/core-team), [Marketing](/roles/marketing-team), [Scanning](/roles/scanning-team) and [Data](/roles/data-team). One person can wear several hats.

## Pitfalls

- Promo codes overlapping a tier discount make prices confusing.
- Inconsistent tracking link names make channels hard to compare.
- Test login codes before sending the team to the gate.
