title: "Followers"
description: "See the people who follow your event."
---

The **Followers** page lets you "view users who are following this event." Open it from **Followers** in the event menu.

## The list

The table shows the people who follow the event, with these columns:

- **Name**: shows a dash if the person has no name on record.
- **Email**
- **Followed At**: the date and time they started following.

Ten followers appear per page. Below the table you see how many are showing, for example "Showing 1 to 10 of 42 followers", with **Previous** and **Next** buttons to move between pages.

If nobody follows the event yet, the page says "When users follow this event, they will appear here."

<Note>
  The list is view-only. There are no actions to take on followers from this page.
</Note>
