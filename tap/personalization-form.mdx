The **Personalization Form** controls the identity questions attendees answer when they personalize or claim a ticket, before their wristband is bound to it. It's listed in the event sidebar and only appears for events with PLVR Tap enabled.

It works like the [registration form](/events/registration-form), but it is a separate form with its own questions.

## Add questions

### Quick add

Click one of the **Quick add** buttons to add a standard question. Each is added as required, and the button disappears once added:

- **Gender**
- **Nationality**
- **Phone Number**
- **Date of Birth**
- **Identity Document**

### Custom questions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Custom question">
    A **Create Custom Question** panel opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the question">
    - **Question** (required)
    - **Description** (optional)
    - **Answer Type** (required): **Text**, **Number**, **Single choice**, or **Multiple choice**
    - **Options** (for the two choice types): add at least one option, using **Add Option**
    - **Required** switch
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The question appears under **Custom Questions**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage your questions

Questions are listed in two tables, **Preset Questions** and **Custom Questions**. Each shows **Question**, **Required**, and **Status**.

- **Required:** use the switch to make a question required or optional.
- **Status:** set a question to **Active** or **Inactive**. Inactive questions are not asked.
- **Order:** drag the handle on the left of a row to reorder questions within its table.

<Note>
Attendees' names and email are always collected as fixed fields, so they aren't listed here.
</Note>
