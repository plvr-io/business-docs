Attendees answer these questions when they personalize or claim a ticket. It is separate from the [registration form](/events/registration-form). Names and email are always collected, so they are not listed.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/tap/tap-personalization-form-page.svg" alt="Personalization Form page with Quick add, Custom question and question rows" />
</Frame>

## Add questions

- **Quick add** (1): adds a standard question (Gender, Nationality, Phone Number, Date of Birth, Identity Document), required by default.
- **Custom question** (2): choose an answer type (text, number, single or multiple choice). Choice types need at least one option.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/tap/tap-personalization-custom-question-panel.svg" alt="Create Custom Question panel with Single choice answer type" />
</Frame>

## Manage questions

On each row (3), toggle **Required**, set **Status** to **Inactive** to stop asking it, or drag the handle to reorder.
