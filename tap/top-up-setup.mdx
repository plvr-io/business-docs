Prepare where and how attendees top up onsite. Find it under **PLVR Tap** in the event sidebar.

## Top-up stations

A station is a physical place where staff add funds, such as a main entrance or VIP area. Stations let you assign staff to a location and track top-ups by location.

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/tap/tap-topup-stations-tab.png | Open PLVR Tap > Top-up Setup, Top-up Stations tab, with two or three stations (one Inactive). Capture the tab bar, Add Top-up Station button and the table. */}

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Top-up Station">Select it to open the form.</Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">A name is required. Description and status (Active by default) are optional.</Step>
  <Step title="Create Station">Edit later with the pencil icon, or change **Status** in the table.</Step>
</Steps>

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/tap/tap-topup-create-station-panel.png | Click Add Top-up Station. Fill in Name "Main Entrance" and a short Description. Capture the panel. */}

<Note>
Inactive stations are hidden in PLVR Onsite but remain available for staff assignment.
</Note>

## Onsite payment methods

These are the options staff pick to record how an attendee paid.

<Warning>
They are for reporting only and do not process payments. Take payment with your own terminal, cash, or other means.
</Warning>

Use **Quick add** for Cash, Card, QR Payment, Bank Transfer, or Other, or **Custom Payment Method** for your own, such as Apple Pay. Only custom methods can be edited. Any method can be set **Active** or **Inactive**.
