Lists top-ups into attendees' wallets and spending at vendors. Find it under **PLVR Tap** in the event sidebar.

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/tap/tap-cashless-activity-overview.png | Open PLVR Tap > Cashless Activity on a Tap event with successful top-ups and spending. Capture the four summary cards, the All / Top-ups / Spending tabs and the table. Add callouts: 1 = summary cards, 2 = tabs, 3 = filters, 4 = Export. */}

The summary cards (1) count successful transactions only. Top-up Amount excludes service fee and tax.

## Filter and export

Switch between **All**, **Top-ups**, and **Spending** (2). Each tab keeps its own filters (3), such as status, top-up channel and station, vendor and sales location, or a search by name, email, or transaction ID.

Cancelled transactions are greyed out. Select **Export** (4) to download a CSV of the current tab with your filters applied.
