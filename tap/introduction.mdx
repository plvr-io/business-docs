title: "PLVR Tap overview"
description: "How cashless wristbands, top-ups, vendors, and approvals fit together."
---

PLVR Tap lets attendees pay by tapping a wristband. Each attendee has an Event Wallet: they top it up, then spend it at vendor stalls. You set it up before the event and monitor it afterwards.

<Note>
PLVR Tap pages only appear for events with PLVR Tap enabled.
</Note>

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Before the event">
    Create [top-up stations](/tap/top-up-setup), set up [vendors](/tap/vendors) and issue login codes, choose what attendees are asked on the [personalization form](/tap/personalization-form), and [approve vendor menus](/tap/approvals).
  </Step>
  <Step title="At the event">
    Staff bind a wristband to each ticket at entry. Attendees top up online, or at a station by cash or card. Vendors scan wristbands to charge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="After the event">
    Review [Cashless activity](/tap/cashless-activity) and the [Spending report](/tap/spending-report), then settle with vendors.
  </Step>
</Steps>
