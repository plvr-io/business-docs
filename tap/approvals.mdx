---
title: "Approvals"
description: "Approve or reject item and category submissions from vendors."
---

When a vendor adds or changes an item or category, it waits here and does not go live on their menu until you approve it. Find it under **PLVR Tap** in the event sidebar.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/tap/tap-approvals-list.svg" alt="Approvals list with filters, Current and Requested values, and review buttons" />
</Frame>

The page shows **Pending** submissions by default. Use the filters (1) to find others by status, type, or vendor. For edits, compare **Current** and **Requested** (2).

## Review a submission

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the details">Click an image to enlarge it.</Step>
  <Step title="Approve or reject (3)">**Approve** applies the submission. **Reject** requires a reason, which the vendor sees.</Step>
</Steps>

## Review in bulk

Tick rows, or the header checkbox, to show **Approve Selected** and **Reject Selected**. You can select up to 50, and a bulk reject uses one reason for all. Submissions that changed in the meantime are skipped.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/tap/tap-approvals-bulk-bar.svg" alt="Approvals with two rows selected and the bulk action bar" />
</Frame>

<Tip>
Submissions from inactive vendors stay reviewable. Check the **Inactive** tag before approving.
</Tip>
