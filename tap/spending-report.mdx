Shows how much was spent at your event. Find it under **PLVR Tap** in the event sidebar.

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/tap/tap-spending-report-by-vendor.png | Open PLVR Tap > Spending Report on an event with sales, By Vendor tab. Add callouts: 1 = By Vendor / By Item tabs, 2 = Total row, 3 = Export Summary and Export All Vendor Reports, 4 = Export Report on a vendor row. */}

Switch between **By Vendor** and **By Item** (1), and use **Search** to filter. The **Total** row (2) covers everything matching your search, not just the current page. Net Sales is Gross Sales minus refunds. Hover a column's info icon for its definition.

## Exports

Exports download as Excel files.

- **Export Summary** (3): the current tab, with your search applied.
- **Export Report** (4): one vendor's settlement report. Use it when settling with that vendor.
- **Export All Vendor Reports** (3): one report per vendor. Files download when ready.
