---
title: "Payment Links"
description: "Charge a buyer a custom amount through a one-time link."
---

**Payment Links** let you charge a specific amount for a specific item, such as a VIP table, without creating a ticket. Create a link, send it to the buyer, and track payment.

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/other/payment-links-list.png | Payment Links with four links: pending, paid, expired and closed. Callouts: 1 Revenue and Successful Payments cards, 2 Create Link, 3 Export CSV, 4 Status column, 5 row menu and refresh button. */}

<Warning>
  Each link can be paid only once and expires 24 hours after creation.
</Warning>

## Create a link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Create Link (2)">
    Enter the **Item** and **Item Price** (above zero). **You Receive** shows your amount after fees.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter buyer details">
    Buyer name and email are required; the receipt goes to that email. **Remarks** are internal only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Save">
    Then share the link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/other/payment-links-create-panel.png | Create Link panel filled in: Item 'VIP Table', price 500, buyer details with 'Use my email' ticked, Remarks 'Table 4'. Include You Receive and Save. */}

## Manage links

Use the row menu (5) to **Copy URL**, **Create QR Code** or **Close Link** (pending links), or **Download Receipt** (paid links). A closed link cannot be reopened. Use the refresh button to check whether a link has been paid.
