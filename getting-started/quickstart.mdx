This page walks you through your first session: signing in, creating an organization if you do not have one yet, and finding your events.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open the portal sign-in page. You will see **Welcome to PLVR Business** with the line "Register or sign in and we'll get started."

    You can continue in one of two ways:

    - **Email.** Enter your email address and select **Continue**. The button stays disabled until the address looks valid. We send a 6-digit verification code to that address.
    - **Apple or Google.** Select the Apple or Google button to sign in with that account.

    There is no separate sign-up step. If you are new, signing in registers you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your verification code">
    If you chose email, the next screen reads **Enter your verification code** and shows the address the code was sent to. Type the 6 digits. The code is checked automatically as soon as you enter the last digit.

    If the code does not arrive, wait for the 60-second countdown to finish and then select **Resend**. To use a different address, select the back arrow.

    After you sign in you see a short welcome message ("Welcome back", or "Welcome home" for a first visit) and are taken into the portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your organization (first time only)">
    If your account does not belong to any organization yet, you are taken straight to **Create your organization**. See [Create an organization](#create-an-organization) below.

    If you already belong to one or more organizations, you land in the one you opened last. If that is no longer available, you land in your newest one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your events">
    You arrive on the **Events** page, which lists your organization's events. Select an event to open its overview and the event menu. See [Events overview](/events/overview).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you are sent to a page that says **Access denied** with a **Back to home** link, your role in the organization does not allow that page. Ask an organization admin or owner to review your role. See [Team and roles](/organization/team).
</Note>

## Create an organization

An organization is the account your events are hosted under. Fill in the form on the **Create your organization** page.

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Organization name** | Required. Up to 40 characters; a counter beside the field shows how many you have used. The name must not already be taken. |
| **Organization page URL** | The address of your public organizer page, shown as your PLVR domain followed by `/organizers/` and your ID. It fills in automatically from your name until you edit it yourself. |
| **Organization logo** | Optional. Upload a JPG, PNG or WebP image up to 5 MB. You can drag a file onto the logo area, and you crop it to a square before it uploads. |

The ID in the page URL must follow these rules:

- 3 to 60 characters.
- Lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens only.
- Must start and end with a letter or number.
- Must not already be taken.
- Some words are reserved and cannot be used, such as `admin`, `support`, `help`, `login` and `plvr`. The form tells you if your ID is reserved.

<Warning>
  You cannot change the page URL after you create the organization. Check it carefully before you select **Create organization**.
</Warning>

At the bottom of the form, **Owner** shows the email address you are signed in with. That account becomes the owner of the new organization. If you signed in with the wrong account, select **Change account**, sign in with the right one, and you will return to this form.

If you already belong to an organization, a **Back to dashboard** button at the top returns you without creating anything. You can also create additional organizations at any time from the account menu. See [Portal tour](/getting-started/portal-tour).

When the organization is created, you see a confirmation and are taken to its workspace.

<Tip>
  Creating an event is not self-service yet. On the Events page, an organization with no events shows a message asking you to contact partners@plvr.io to create one. Selecting the address copies it.
</Tip>
