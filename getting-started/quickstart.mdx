---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "A short path to launching your first event on the PLVR Business Portal, from event page to first ticket sale."
---

This page assumes you already have an account and an organization. If not, start with [Onboarding](/getting-started/onboarding), which covers signing up, creating your organization, your profile and your team.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your event">
    Select **Events** in the header, then select your event. If you do not have one yet, see [Create your first event](/getting-started/onboarding#create-your-first-event).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the event page">
    Open **Event Editor**, review each section, and select **Save Event**. See [Event editor](/events/event-editor).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your tickets">
    Open **Tickets** and review your ticket types and prices. See [Ticket management](/tickets/ticket-management).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the event live">
    On the **Events** page, use the status menu on the event's row to choose **Public**. See [Event status](/events/overview#event-status).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Promote it">
    Create a [promo code](/promotions/promo-codes) or a [tracking link](/promotions/tracking-links) to share, and see which channels bring buyers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch sales come in">
    Follow sales on the event's [Overview](/events/overview) and in the [Ticket report](/tickets/ticket-report).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Before you set the event to **Public**, run through the checklist in [Before you go live](/getting-started/onboarding#before-you-go-live).
</Note>
