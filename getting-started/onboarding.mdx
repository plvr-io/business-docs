A quick path from nothing to a ready organization. Anyone can create an account.

## Set up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open the [Business Portal](https://business.plvr.io) and sign in with email, Apple or Google. Your account is created the first time you sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your organization">
    Enter a name, page URL and optional logo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your profile">
    Open **Organization** and fill in what attendees see on your public page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your team">
    Open **Team**, select **Add**, and give each person a role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first event">
    Contact the PLVR partners team.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Create your organization

An organization is the account your events are hosted under. You land on this form after your first sign-in. To add another organization later, use **Create organization** in the account menu.

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/getting-started/onboarding-create-organization-form.png | The 'Create organization' page with a name typed in (e.g. 'Neon Nights'), the Organization page URL auto-filled, the logo area visible, and the Owner row with 'Change account' at the bottom. Add numbered callouts: 1 = name, 2 = page URL, 3 = logo, 4 = Owner row. */}

- **Name (1):** up to 40 characters, must be unique.
- **Page URL (2):** 3 to 60 lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens, starting and ending with a letter or number. Reserved words such as `admin`, `support` and `help` are not allowed.
- **Logo (3):** optional JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB.
- **Owner (4):** the account you are signed in with becomes the owner. Select **Change account** if it is the wrong one.

<Warning>
  You cannot change the page URL after the organization is created. Check it first.
</Warning>

## Complete your profile

Open **Organization** in the header, fill in your logo, name and **About My Organization**, then select **Save**. See [Organization settings](/organization/settings) for every option.

## Add your team

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/getting-started/onboarding-team-add-button.png | Team Management page (Team in the header) with a few members listed; highlight the Add button at the top. Crop to the page title, Add button and the top of the member table. */}

| Role | Access |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Full access to PLVR Business and PLVR Scan. |
| **Manager** | PLVR Business and PLVR Scan, except Team Management. |
| **Scanner** | PLVR Scan only. |

Give each person the lowest role that fits. Door staff only need **Scanner**. See [Team and roles](/organization/team).

## Create your first event

Creating an event is not self-service yet. On the empty **Events** page, email the partners team at partners@plvr.io to get your event set up. It then appears in your list. See [Events overview](/events/overview).

## Before you go live

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build the event page">
    In **Event Editor**, review the page and select **Save Event**. Nothing is published until you do. See [Event editor](/events/event-editor).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up tickets">
    Check types and prices under **Tickets**. See [Ticket management](/tickets/ticket-management).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add checkout questions (optional)">
    Use the [Registration form](/events/registration-form).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the event status">
    Set **Public**, **Private** or **Inactive** on the **Events** page. See [Event status](/events/overview#event-status).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview as an attendee">
    Select **View event** to check the public page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your door team">
    Add scanning staff as **Scanner** on the [Team page](/organization/team).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common problems

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| **Access denied** | Your role does not allow that page, or you are in a different organization. Select **Back to home**, check the account menu, or ask an owner to review your role. |
| Can't find my organization, or asked to create a new one | You signed in with a different account than your team used. Log out and sign in with the original email or provider. |
| Added to a team but don't see their organization | Sign in with the email they added, then switch organization in the account menu. |
| Name or page URL already taken, or reserved | Make it more distinctive, for example add your city. |
| Logo rejected | Use JPG, PNG or WebP under 5 MB. |
| Verification code didn't arrive | Check spam, wait for the countdown, then select **Resend**. Check the address for typos. |
| Apple or Google sign-in failed | Try again, or use email. |
| Need a different page URL | It is fixed. Create a new organization from the account menu. |
