**Audience** (in the header) shows attendee loyalty across your whole organization, not a single event. Retention Rate is returning attendees as a percentage of total unique attendees.

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/organization/audience-headline-and-charts.png | Audience page for an organization with several events and returning attendees. Full-page capture: three headline cards and the three charts (Audience Distribution, Returning Attendees Growth, Monthly Active Attendees). */}

## Returning attendees

The **Returning Attendees** table lists everyone who has bought tickets to 2 or more of your events. Select **Export CSV** to download it.

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/organization/audience-returning-attendees-table.png | Audience page scrolled to the Returning Attendees table with several rows. Add callout 1 on the Export CSV button. */}

<Note>
  The export contains names and email addresses. Share it only with team members who need it.
</Note>
