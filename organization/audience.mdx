The **Audience** page shows who is attending your organization's events and how loyal they are. Open it from **Audience** in the header. Its subtitle reads "Understand who is attending your events and how loyal they are."

The figures cover your whole organization, not a single event.

## Headline numbers

Three cards sit at the top:

- **Total Unique Attendees**: the number of different people who have attended your events.
- **Returning Attendees**: how many of them have attended more than one of your events.
- **Retention Rate**: returning attendees as a percentage of total unique attendees.

## Returning Attendees list

Below the charts, the **Returning Attendees** table lists everyone who has purchased tickets to 2 or more events, with the total shown beside the heading. Columns:

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The attendee's name, or a dash if none is on record. |
| **Email** | Their email address. |
| **Events** | How many of your events they have attended. |
| **First Purchase** | The date of their first ticket purchase. |
| **Became Returning** | The date they became a returning attendee. |

If there are none yet, the table shows "No returning attendees yet."

## Export to CSV

Select **Export CSV** above the table to download the returning attendees list as a CSV file. While it prepares you see "Exporting...". The file is named with your organization ID and today's date.

<Note>
  The export contains attendees' personal details such as names and email addresses. Share it only with people in your team who need it.
</Note>
