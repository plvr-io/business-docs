title: "Team and roles"
description: "Add team members, set their roles, and remove them."
---

**Team** (in the header) controls who can work in your organization.

<Note>
  Only owners and admins can open this page.
</Note>

| Role | Access |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Everything in PLVR Business and PLVR Scan. |
| **Manager** | PLVR Business and PLVR Scan, except Team Management. |
| **Scanner** | PLVR Scan only (ticket scanning). |

The **owner** (whoever created the organization) is listed but cannot be edited or removed.

## Add a member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Add (1)">
    Enter their name, email and role. All are required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Save">
    The member appears in the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/organization/team-add-member-window.svg" alt="Add a Team Member window with the Role dropdown open" />
</Frame>

## Change a role or remove a member

Use the pencil (2) to change a role; name and email are locked. Use the bin (3) to remove, then confirm.

<Warning>
  If you give yourself a role that cannot access Team, you may lose access to this page immediately.
</Warning>
