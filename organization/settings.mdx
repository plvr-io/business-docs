---

**Organization** (in the header) controls how your organization appears on its public organizer page.

<Note>
  Only owners, admins and managers can open this page.
</Note>

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/organization/settings-profile-top.png | Organization Settings from the top, with sample data. Callouts: 1 logo, 2 Organization name, 3 Page URL with copy button, 4 About My Organization. */}

- **Logo (1):** PNG, WebP, JPG or JPEG, up to 4 MB.
- **Name (2):** required, up to 40 characters.
- **Page URL (3):** set when the organization was created and cannot be changed here.
- **About (4):** shown on your public page.

## Optional sections

Expand any of these to fill them in: Featured Event, Website & Social Media, Community Groups, Music, Venue Information, Public Metrics and Atmosphere Color.

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/organization/settings-optional-sections.png | Settings scrolled to the optional sections, all collapsed except Website & Social Media (expanded). */}

- The website must start with `http://` or `https://`.
- Community Groups allows one group only: WhatsApp or Telegram. Saving one clears the other.
- Custom Atmosphere Colors must be confirmed with **Apply Color**.

## Save

Select **Save** at the bottom. If you leave with unsaved changes, you are asked to confirm.

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/organization/settings-save-confirmation.png | Bottom of Organization Settings after clicking Save, showing the Save button and the success toast. */}
