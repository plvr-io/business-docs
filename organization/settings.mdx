**Organization** (in the header) controls how your organization appears on its public organizer page.

<Note>
  Only owners, admins and managers can open this page.
</Note>

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/organization/settings-profile-top.png | Organization Settings from the top, with sample data. Callouts: 1 logo, 2 Organization name, 3 Page URL with copy button, 4 About My Organization. */}

- **Logo (1):** PNG, WebP, JPG or JPEG, up to 4 MB.
- **Name (2):** required, up to 40 characters.
- **Page URL (3):** set when the organization was created and cannot be changed here.
- **About (4):** shown on your public page.

## Optional sections

Expand a section to add details to your public organizer page.

- **Featured Event:** select an event from the dropdown to highlight it, then save.
- **Website & Social Media:** add publicly accessible links to your website, Instagram, X / Twitter, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube. The website must start with `http://` or `https://`.
- **Community Groups:** add one WhatsApp or Telegram invite link. Saving one clears the other.
- **Music:** add SoundCloud or Spotify artist or playlist links.
- **Venue Information:** select the checkbox if your organization operates a physical venue.
- **Public Metrics:** use **Display Number of Attendees** to show or hide attendee numbers.
- **Atmosphere Color:** choose your organization's color. For a custom color, select **Apply Color**, then save.

## Save

Select **Save** at the bottom. If you leave with unsaved changes, you are asked to confirm.
