**Onsite Operations** is your control room. Its **Command Center** shows what needs attention and lets you search any guest. Ticket-only events admit by QR scan or manual check-in. Tap events admit by wristband and add **Wristbands** and **Cashless**. See [Command Center](/onsite/command-center).

## Before doors open

<Check>Every team member has an **Active** code in **Onsite Staff**, top-up staff tied to the right station.</Check>
<Check>Spare wristbands added and **Approvals** empty (Tap events).</Check>
<Check>Door sales poster in place, if selling at the door. See [Door sales](/promotions/door-sales).</Check>
<Check>The desk knows where **Search** and **+ New service case** are.</Check>

## Who does what

| Job | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Gate team: admit guests | [Tickets & Entry](/onsite/tickets) |
| Top-up team (Tap) | [Top-up setup](/tap/top-up-setup) |
| Desk: resolve entry and wristband problems | [Search](/onsite/search) |
| Supervisor: restricted wristband actions | [Wristbands](/onsite/wristbands) |
| Event lead: watch Command Center, review escalations | [Service cases](/onsite/cases) |

Each code's **Role** is set in **Onsite Staff**. Keep the Command Center open all day. It refreshes about every 30 seconds, and the **Attention queue** is ordered by risk, so work from the top.

## Common situations

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A guest cannot get in">
    **Search** by ticket ID, email, order number or wristband, check **Why it matched**, then **Resolve** and follow the recommended action (such as **Admit with an override** or **Allow re-entry**). See [Tickets & Entry](/onsite/tickets).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Wristband lost, broken or not working">
    **Replace the wristband** (balance unaffected), **Report the wristband lost or stolen**, or **Freeze the wristband**. To stop all spending use **Freeze the wallet** on the profile. An **Ownership conflict** goes to a supervisor. See [Wristbands](/onsite/wristbands).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A top-up did not arrive">
    Open **Cashless**, search the guest, and **Investigate** the unresolved top-up. Do not tell the guest no money was taken, and do not ask them to pay again. If stuck, **Open a payment review case**. The console cannot refund. See [Cashless](/onsite/cashless).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Balance needs fixing">
    On the participant's **Cashless** tab: **Correct the balance** or **Issue a reward credit**. Both are recorded against your name. See [Participants](/onsite/participants).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An approved guest is not on the list">
    In **Guests**, search and **Check in**, or **Add an approved guest** and record who approved. Never admit on a name match alone. See [Guests](/onsite/guests).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Someone wants to buy at the door">
    Point them to the poster. They scan it and buy on their phone.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## When you cannot fix it

Never turn someone away with nothing recorded. Use **+ New service case**: describe the problem, **Add evidence** (a reference number, named confirmation or note; verbal agreement is not evidence), then **Send for review** if you cannot safely act, or close it as resolved or cannot be resolved. See [Service cases](/onsite/cases).

## End of the day

<Steps>
  <Step title="Clear the queue">
    Work **Service cases** > **Still open** down to nothing, or a clear next step each.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn off codes">
    Set finished staff and vendor codes to **Inactive**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Note the numbers">
    **Tickets issued**, **Entered** and the cashless cards, for [After the event](/guides/after-the-event).
  </Step>
</Steps>
