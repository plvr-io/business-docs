On the day, **Onsite Operations** is your control room. Open it from the event menu. Its first screen, the **Command Center**, shows what needs attention and lets you search for any guest.

<Note>
What you see depends on the event type. A **Ticket-only event** has entry by QR scan or manual check-in, and no wristbands or cashless wallet. A **Tap event** admits guests by wristband and also shows the **Wristbands** and **Cashless** workspaces. See [Command Center](/onsite/command-center).
</Note>

## Before doors open

<Check>Every team member has an **Active** login code under **Onsite Staff**, and top-up staff are tied to the right station.</Check>
<Check>Spare wristbands are added (Tap events) and the **Approvals** list has no pending items.</Check>
<Check>Door sales poster is printed and in place, if you are selling at the door. See [Door sales](/promotions/door-sales).</Check>
<Check>Someone on the desk has opened the **Command Center** and knows where **Search** and **+ New service case** are.</Check>

## Who does what on shift

Each person signs in with a login code, and each code has a **Role** chosen when you create it under **Onsite Staff**. The **Role** list describes what each role is for, so pick the one that fits the job and use the **Note** to record who holds the code. Plan your shifts around jobs like these.

| Job | What they do | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gate team | Admit guests, check tickets | [Tickets & Entry](/onsite/tickets) |
| Top-up team | Add money to wallets at their assigned station (Tap events) | [Top-up setup](/tap/top-up-setup) |
| Desk operators | Search, resolve entry and wristband problems, record cases | [Search](/onsite/search) |
| Supervisor | Handles restricted wristband actions and decisions the desk cannot make | [Wristbands](/onsite/wristbands) |
| You or the event lead | Watch the Command Center, review escalated cases | [Service cases](/onsite/cases) |

<Tip>
Keep the Command Center open on one screen all day. It refreshes about every 30 seconds.
</Tip>

## Watch the floor

On the Command Center, check these regularly:

- **Open service cases** and **Blocked from entry** counts.
- **Unresolved top-ups** (Tap events), which counts money that may have been taken without the balance going up.
- The **Operational health** banner, which appears as **Degraded** or **Incident** only when something is wrong.
- **Floor alerts**, which appear when several failures cluster together.
- The **Attention queue**, ordered by risk and then by waiting time. Work from the top.

## Handle common situations

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A guest cannot get in">
    1. Find them with **Search** by Ticket ID, email, order number or wristband.
    2. Read **Why it matched** on the result. If it says the match was the purchaser, the ticket belongs to someone else. Open the right person.
    3. Select **Resolve**. The page tells you in plain words what is wrong and gives a "Say to the participant" sentence.
    4. Use the recommended action, such as **Admit with an override** (needs a written account of what you verified and the confirmation tick box). Use **Allow re-entry** when someone returns after leaving.

    See [Tickets & Entry](/onsite/tickets).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Wristband lost, broken or not working">
    - **Replace the wristband** retires the old band and binds a new one to the same ticket. The balance is not affected.
    - **Report the wristband lost or stolen** stops the band immediately.
    - **Freeze the wristband** stops one band. To stop all spending, use **Freeze the wallet** on the participant's profile.
    - If a band is already bound to someone else, you will see an **Ownership conflict**. Use **Investigate ownership** and let a supervisor decide.

    See [Wristbands](/onsite/wristbands).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A top-up did not arrive">
    1. Open **Cashless** from the Command Center and search the guest's email or reference.
    2. Select **Investigate** on the unresolved top-up to see where it broke.
    3. Do not tell the guest no money was taken unless you have checked, and do not ask them to pay again.
    4. If you cannot settle it, select **Open a payment review case**.

    The console does not refund or move money between wallets. See [Cashless](/onsite/cashless).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Balance needs fixing">
    On the participant's **Cashless** tab, use **Correct the balance** for a misrecorded amount, or **Issue a reward credit** as goodwill. Both ask for details and are recorded against your name. See [Participants](/onsite/participants).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A VIP or approved guest is not on the list">
    Open **Guests**. Search by name or email, and select **Check in**. If they are not listed, use **Add an approved guest** and record **Who approved this?**. Never admit on a name match alone. See [Guests](/onsite/guests).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Someone wants to buy a ticket at the door">
    Point them to the door sales poster. When they scan the QR code they land on your event page and buy on their phone. Watch **Tickets Sold** on the **Door Sales** page.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## When you cannot fix it: service cases

Nobody should be turned away with nothing recorded. If no action solves the problem, record a service case with **+ New service case** (or **Record a service case** on a profile).

1. Pick **What kind of problem?** and describe **What happened at the desk?**.
2. If the guest could not be found, type what they showed you exactly as they gave it.
3. Select **Add evidence**: a **Reference number**, a **Named confirmation**, or a **Note**. Verbal agreement alone is not evidence.
4. Choose **Send for review** and the kind of review (**Identity dispute**, **Payment review**, **Ticket entitlement review** or **Operational incident**) if you cannot safely act.
5. Conclude with **Close as resolved** and what the guest was told, or **Conclude: cannot be resolved** with the reason.

See [Service cases](/onsite/cases).

## End of the day

<Steps>
  <Step title="Clear the queue">
    Open **Service cases** and work through **Still open** until it is empty or each remaining case has a clear next step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn off codes">
    In **Onsite Staff**, set codes for finished shifts to **Inactive**, and do the same for vendor login codes in **Vendor Setup**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Note the numbers">
    Note **Tickets issued** and **Entered** from **Tickets & Entry**, and the cashless cards, for the after-event review. See [After the event](/guides/after-the-event).
  </Step>
</Steps>
