## 1. Review ticket sales

On the event **Overview**, check the totals, **Trend**, **Performance Insights** and **Ticket Breakdown** to see which tiers sold out. For detail, open the [Ticket Report](/tickets/ticket-report) and **Export**. See [Events overview](/events/overview).

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/guides/after-event-overview.png | Event Overview after sales. Capture the totals, Trend chart, Performance Insights and Ticket Breakdown table (scroll-stitch if needed). */}

## 2. Check attendance

In Onsite Operations > **Tickets & Entry**, compare **Tickets issued** with **Entered**. See [Tickets & Entry](/onsite/tickets).

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/guides/after-event-tickets-and-entry.png | Onsite Operations > Tickets & Entry with entries recorded. Capture 'Tickets issued' vs 'Entered' and 'Recent entry activity'. */}

## 3. Cashless (Tap events)

- [Cashless activity](/tap/cashless-activity): top-ups and spending. **Export** a CSV.
- [Spending report](/tap/spending-report) > **By Vendor**: **Export Report** per vendor for settlement, or **Export All Vendor Reports**.
- Onsite Operations > **Cashless**: use **Unresolved only** to confirm no top-up is waiting, and check open **Service cases**.

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/guides/after-event-spending-report.png | PLVR Tap > Spending Report, By Vendor tab with 3 vendors. Callouts: 1 Net Sales column, 2 Export Report on a row, 3 Export All Vendor Reports. */}

<Note>
**Top-up Amount** excludes service fee and tax.
</Note>

## 4. Measure promotions

| Question | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Which codes were used? | [Promo Codes](/promotions/promo-codes): **Uses/Limit**, **Revenue**, **Cost** |
| Which channels sent visitors? | [Tracking Links](/promotions/tracking-links) > **Link History** |
| Which partners sold? | Ticket Report: **Affiliate Code** |
| Which emails worked? | [Campaigns](/promotions/campaigns): **Open Rate**, **Revenue** |
| Door sales? | [Door Sales](/promotions/door-sales) |

## 5. Follow up

- **Followers** is view-only.
- **Audience** (header) shows returning attendees and retention across events, with **Export CSV**. See [Audience](/organization/audience).
- Email **Ticket Holders** to say thanks and announce what is next.

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/guides/after-event-audience.png | Audience page from the header. Capture the three stat cards and the Export CSV button. */}

<Warning>
Exports contain names and emails. Share only as needed.
</Warning>
