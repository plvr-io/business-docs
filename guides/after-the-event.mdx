Once the doors close, the portal helps you answer four questions: how did we sell, how did people spend, which promotions worked, and who do we talk to next.

## 1. Review ticket sales

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start at the Overview">
    Open your event's **Overview** page. Check **Total Revenue**, **Tickets Sold**, **Page Views** and **Conversion Rate**, and use the **Trend** chart to see when sales spiked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read Performance Insights">
    **Revenue Source** splits revenue into tickets, add-ons and payment links. **Sales Attribution** splits tickets into **Full Price Sales**, **Promo Sales** and **Comp Tickets**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check each ticket type">
    The **Ticket Breakdown** table shows **Sold / Total (%)** and **Revenue** for every ticket type, so you can see which tiers sold out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export the detail">
    Open the **Ticket Report**, apply a date range, session or item filter if needed, and select **Export** to download a CSV.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [Events overview](/events/overview) and [Ticket report](/tickets/ticket-report).

## 2. Check attendance

On Onsite Operations, open **Tickets & Entry** and review **Tickets issued** against **Entered**. The **Recent entry activity** list shows what the team did at the gates. For Tap events, you can also check **Wristbands** for any that were lost or stopped.

## 3. Review cashless activity (Tap events)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Cashless Activity">
    Open **PLVR Tap > Cashless Activity**. The summary cards show **Top-up Transactions**, **Top-up Amount**, **Spending Transactions** and **Spending Amount**. Filter by **Status**, **Top-up Channel**, **Top-up Station**, **Vendor** or **Sales Location**, then **Export** a CSV of the current tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spending Report">
    Open **PLVR Tap > Spending Report**. **By Vendor** shows **Gross Sales**, **Refunded Amount** and **Net Sales** per vendor. **By Item** shows what sold best.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Settle with vendors">
    On the **By Vendor** tab, use **Export Report** on a vendor row to download that vendor's settlement report, or **Export All Vendor Reports** for the whole event.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look for loose ends">
    In Onsite Operations, open **Cashless** and use **Unresolved only** to make sure no top-up is still waiting. Review **Service cases** for anything left open.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**Top-up Amount** excludes service fee and tax. See [Cashless activity](/tap/cashless-activity) and [Spending report](/tap/spending-report).
</Note>

## 4. Measure your promotions

| Question | Where to look |
| --- | --- |
| Which codes were used, and what did they cost? | **Promo Codes**: **Uses/Limit**, **Revenue**, **Cost**. **View Usage** shows each order. |
| Which channels sent visitors? | **Tracking Links > Link History**: **Clicks** and **Unique Clicks**. |
| Which partners sold tickets? | **Ticket Report**: the **Affiliate Code** column. |
| Which emails performed? | **Campaigns**: **Open Rate**, **Click Rate** and **Revenue**. |
| How did door sales do? | **Door Sales**: **Total Scan**, **Tickets Sold**, **Revenue**. |

<Tip>
Write down what worked while it is fresh. Next time you plan a campaign, start from [Promote your event](/guides/promote-your-event) and reuse the winning channels.
</Tip>

## 5. Follow up with your audience

- **Followers** lists everyone who follows the event, with **Followed At**. It is view-only, so use it to see how big your following is.
- **Audience** (in the header) shows **Total Unique Attendees**, **Returning Attendees** and **Retention Rate** across all your events. Use **Export CSV** to download the returning attendees list.
- To thank attendees or announce what is next, send an email campaign to **Ticket Holders** or to your own uploaded list.

<Warning>
Exports contain names and email addresses. Share them only with team members who need them.
</Warning>
