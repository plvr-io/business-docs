---
title: "Cashless festival setup"
description: "Set up PLVR Tap end to end, in the order that avoids rework."
---

Finish this at least a few days before doors open: vendors need time to build menus and you need time to approve them.

<Note>
**PLVR Tap** and **Personalization Form** only appear for Tap events. See the [PLVR Tap overview](/tap/introduction).
</Note>

| Step | Page | Done when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Top-up Setup** | Stations and payment methods exist |
| 2 | **Personalization Form** | Questions chosen |
| 3 | **Vendor Setup** | Vendors, locations, items, login codes exist |
| 4 | **Approvals** | Nothing pending |
| 5 | **Onsite Staff** | Every team member has a code |
| 6 | **Wristbands** | Spares added |

## 1. Top-up stations and payment methods

In **PLVR Tap > Top-up Setup**, add a station for every place staff will add money (for example Main Entrance, VIP Area), and only the payment methods your team will accept. See [Top-up setup](/tap/top-up-setup).

<Warning>
Payment methods are for reporting only. Staff must take the money with your own cash box or card terminal.
</Warning>

## 2. Personalization Form

Choose what attendees answer when they personalize or claim a ticket, before a wristband is bound. Mark **Required** only what you really need. Separate from the checkout [Registration form](/events/registration-form). See [Personalization form](/tap/personalization-form).

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/guides/cashless-personalization-form.png | Personalization Form with Phone Number (Required), Date of Birth, one custom question and one Inactive question. Callouts: 1 Quick add, 2 Custom question, 3 drag handles. */}

## 3. Vendors

In **Vendor Setup**, each stall or bar is a vendor. Per vendor: add **Sales Locations**, then **Items** (each needs a location and a price above zero, or let the vendor submit them for approval), then generate **Login Codes**, one per person on shift with a **Note** of who holds it. See [Vendors](/tap/vendors).

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/guides/cashless-vendor-setup.png | Vendor Setup > 'Noodle Bar'. Callouts: 1 tabs (Sales Locations, Items, Login Codes), 2 Login Codes with Notes like 'Alex - Vendor Manager'. Two crops fine (add -items suffix for the second). */}

## 4. Approvals

Vendor items and edits stay off the menu until you approve them. Review each, then **Approve**, or **Reject** with a reason the vendor sees. Tick rows for bulk actions. See [Approvals](/tap/approvals).

<Warning>
A vendor cannot sell a pending menu. Aim for an empty Pending list the day before.
</Warning>

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/guides/cashless-approvals.png | Approvals with a few pending submissions, one edit showing Current vs Requested. Callouts: 1 checkboxes, 2 Approve Selected / Reject Selected, 3 menu badge. */}

## 5. Onsite staff

In **Onsite Staff** select **Add Staff**. For the top-up team choose **Top-up staff** and a **Top-up Station** (one batch per station). Create codes with **CSV Import** or **Manual Creation**, and set codes **Inactive** when a shift ends. See [Onsite staff](/onsite/onsite-staff).

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/guides/cashless-onsite-staff.png | Onsite Staff > Add Staff with 'Top-up staff' role and a Top-up Station selected. Callouts: 1 role, 2 Top-up Station, 3 CSV Import / Manual Creation. */}

## 6. Wristbands

In Onsite Operations > **Wristbands**, **+ Add wristbands**: one ID per line (up to 200), with a **Note**. A band reported as newly registered when you expected an existing one usually means a mistyped ID. See [Wristbands](/onsite/wristbands).

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/guides/cashless-add-wristbands.png | Wristbands > '+ Add wristbands' with 3 IDs and a Note, after Add, showing the result lines. */}
