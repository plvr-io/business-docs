Aim for a public event page, tickets people can buy, and a team that knows its job.

## Checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get started">
    Set up your organization and invite your team. See [Onboarding](/getting-started/onboarding), [Organization settings](/organization/settings) and [Team and roles](/organization/team).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the event page">
    Fill in the [Event editor](/events/event-editor).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up tickets">
    Prices, quantities, sale windows and add-ons in [Ticket management](/tickets/ticket-management). New to tickets? Read [Ticket concepts](/tickets/concepts). Need free tickets? See [Complimentary tickets](/tickets/comp-tickets).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose checkout questions">
    Decide what buyers answer in the [Registration form](/events/registration-form). If you approve guests, see [RSVP](/events/rsvp).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go through the launch checklist">
    Follow the [launch checklist](/guides/launch-checklist) before you open sales.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Promote">
    Create [Tracking links](/promotions/tracking-links) and [Promo codes](/promotions/promo-codes), enroll [Affiliates](/promotions/affiliates), and send [Email campaigns](/promotions/campaigns). See the [promote your event guide](/guides/promote-your-event).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional: set up PLVR Tap">
    For cashless events only. Start with the [PLVR Tap overview](/tap/introduction), then [Top-up setup](/tap/top-up-setup), [Vendors](/tap/vendors) and the [cashless festival setup guide](/guides/cashless-festival-setup).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare event day">
    Create login codes in [Onsite staff](/onsite/onsite-staff) and set up [Door sales](/promotions/door-sales) if you will sell at the gate. Read the [event day runbook](/guides/event-day-runbook).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who does this

Mostly the [Core team](/roles/core-team), with the [Marketing team](/roles/marketing-team) on promotion. For your event type, see [small events](/organizers/small-events), [mid-size events](/organizers/mid-size-events), [festivals](/organizers/festivals) or [recurring community events](/organizers/recurring-community).

## What done looks like

- The event page is complete and tickets are on sale.
- Checkout questions are chosen.
- Every promotion channel has its own link or code.
- Staff have login codes and know their jobs.
- For cashless events, stations and vendors are ready.
