## Goal

Get guests in smoothly and solve problems quickly.

## Checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the doors">
    Check guests in and admit them. See [Guests](/onsite/guests) and the [event day runbook](/guides/event-day-runbook).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sell at the door">
    Guests without tickets scan the QR code from your poster. See [Door sales](/promotions/door-sales).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the desk">
    Start from the [Command Center](/onsite/command-center). Use [Search](/onsite/search) to find someone, [Tickets & Entry](/onsite/tickets) when a guest cannot get in, and [Participants](/onsite/participants) to see everything about one person.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wristbands (PLVR Tap events)">
    Bind, take off, freeze or replace bands. See [Wristbands](/onsite/wristbands).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Handle cashless issues">
    Resolve unresolved top-ups in [Cashless](/onsite/cashless) and follow activity in [Cashless activity](/tap/cashless-activity). Check [Approvals](/tap/approvals) if a request is waiting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record problems">
    Log them as [Service cases](/onsite/cases).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Shift jobs

Each person signs in with a login code and a role: check-in, add-on check, binding, top-up or supervisor. See [Onsite staff](/onsite/onsite-staff) and the [Scanning team](/roles/scanning-team) page. Some wristband actions are Restricted and need a supervisor.

## Who does this

The [Scanning team](/roles/scanning-team), with the [Core team](/roles/core-team) on call for decisions and the [Data team](/roles/data-team) watching sales and cashless activity.

## What done looks like

- Everyone with a valid ticket got in.
- Cases are recorded and resolved or handed over.
- No top-ups are left unresolved.
- Login codes are set to **Inactive** after the shift.
