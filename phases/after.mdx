## Goal

Know what happened, settle up, and bring people back for the next event.

## Checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review sales">
    Read the [Events overview](/events/overview) and export the [Ticket report](/tickets/ticket-report).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check spending (PLVR Tap events)">
    Use the [Spending report](/tap/spending-report) for vendor and item totals and settlement, and [Cashless activity](/tap/cashless-activity) for line-by-line detail.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check payment links">
    Review anything paid through [Payment links](/other/payment-links).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare promotions">
    See which [Promo codes](/promotions/promo-codes), [Tracking links](/promotions/tracking-links) and [Email campaigns](/promotions/campaigns) worked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Learn about your audience">
    Open [Audience](/organization/audience) to see who attended and how many return.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow up">
    Check [Followers](/events/followers) and email them with [Email campaigns](/promotions/campaigns).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close out">
    Work through the [after the event guide](/guides/after-the-event).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who does this

The [Data team](/roles/data-team) for reports and spending, and the [Marketing team](/roles/marketing-team) for promo review and follow-up. The [Core team](/roles/core-team) signs off.

## What done looks like

- Reports are exported and vendors are settled.
- You know which channels worked.
- Followers and attendees have heard from you.
- Notes for the next event are written down.

Planning the next one? Go back to [Before the event](/phases/before).
