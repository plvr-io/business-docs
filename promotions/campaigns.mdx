---
title: "Email Campaigns"
description: "Email ticket holders, interested followers, or your own list, and track results."
---

Send email blasts about your event and follow opens, clicks, and revenue on the same page. Campaigns send as soon as you confirm. There are no drafts, and sent campaigns cannot be edited.

<Note>
  Each event has a weekly limit on blasts. **Remaining Blasts** shows what is left, and sending is disabled at zero.
</Note>

## Create and send a campaign

Select **Send emails >**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name it">The campaign name is for you only. Recipients do not see it.</Step>
  <Step title="Add recipients">Choose who gets it (see below).</Step>
  <Step title="Write the email">Enter a subject and content.</Step>
  <Step title="Send a test">Select **Send test** to email a preview to yourself.</Step>
  <Step title="Send">Select **Send email**, check the recipient count, then confirm.</Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Subjects or content with restricted words are blocked, for tests too. The message lists the words to change.
</Warning>

## Choose recipients

Only the tab you are on when you save is used.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Recommendation">
    Pick one or more groups: **Ticket Holders**, **Pending RSVPs**, or **Interested (No Purchase)**. Empty groups cannot be selected.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Custom">
    Upload a CSV (use **Download Template**; it needs an **Email** column) or type addresses one by one. Invalid and duplicate addresses are skipped.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/promotions/campaigns-add-recipients.png | Click Add Recipients. Capture the window with both tabs, Recommendation selected, Ticket Holders ticked, and one empty group greyed out. */}

An estimate of recipients and send time appears as soon as you choose.

## The email editor

Format text, insert images, or use **Insert Name** to greet each recipient by name. **Add Button** adds a call-to-action button at the bottom of the email.

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/promotions/campaigns-add-button.png | Click Add Button. Capture the 'Add Button' window with Button Text 'Get tickets', Link to 'Event Page' and Save. */}

<Tip>
  Set the button's **Link to** as **Event Page** so clicks and revenue are tracked in the campaigns list. A **Custom URL** cannot be tracked.
</Tip>
