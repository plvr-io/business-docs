title: "Tracking Links"
description: "Build trackable links and QR codes per marketing channel and see their clicks."
---

Share your event page through channels like Instagram, a newsletter, or a flyer, and see which ones bring visitors.

## Build a link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a channel">Pick a category, then a sub-channel (1).</Step>
  <Step title="Name the campaign">Required. Use consistent names across channels and underscores instead of spaces (2).</Step>
  <Step title="Add an affiliate ID (optional)">Tracks referrals from a specific partner (3).</Step>
  <Step title="Save">Select **Copy** to use the link, and **Save to History** (4) to keep it and start counting clicks.</Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/promotions/tracking-links-link-builder.svg" alt="Link Builder with Channel (1), Campaign (2), Affiliate ID (3) and Save to History (4)" />
</Frame>

<Note>
  Only saved links get a short URL and click counts. Save every link you plan to share.
</Note>

## Link History

Saved links appear here with total and unique clicks. Use the row buttons to copy the short URL or open its **QR Code**, which you can copy or download as a PNG.
