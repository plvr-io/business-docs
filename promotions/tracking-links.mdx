title: "Tracking Links"
description: "Build trackable links and QR codes for each marketing channel and see how many clicks each one gets."
---

**Tracking Links** let you share your event page through different channels, such as Instagram, a newsletter, or a flyer, and see which ones bring visitors. Each link you save gets its own short URL and click counts.

## Build a link

Use the **Link Builder** at the top of the page. Select the lightbulb icon for tips: use consistent campaign names across channels, avoid spaces (use underscores), and use an affiliate ID to track partner performance.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a channel">
    Under **Channel**, pick a category, then a sub-channel. Until you pick a sub-channel, you will see "Please select a sub-channel".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the campaign">
    Enter a **Campaign** name, for example `spring_sale_2024`. This is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an affiliate ID (optional)">
    **Affiliate ID**, for example `partner123`, lets you track referrals from a specific partner in orders and ticket sales.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use or save the link">
    The **Generated URL** panel shows your link and how many extra parameters it includes. Select **Copy** to copy it, or the open button to preview it. Select **Save to History** to keep the link and start counting clicks.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Link History

Saved links appear in **Link History**, with the total number of links next to the title. The table shows:

- **Label**: the link's name, with its short URL underneath.
- **Destination URL**: where the link sends visitors.
- **Channel**: the channel and source.
- **Clicks**: total clicks.
- **Unique Clicks**: unique visitors.
- **Last Click**: when the link was last clicked.

Use the buttons at the end of each row to copy the short URL (**Copy Short URL**) or open its **QR Code**. In the QR Code window you can select **Copy QR Code** or **Download PNG**. The page also shows **Total Clicks** and **Total Unique Clicks** across your links, and links are listed 20 per page.

<Note>
  Only saved links have their own short URL and click counts. Select **Save to History** for every link you plan to share.
</Note>
