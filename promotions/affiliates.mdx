---
title: "Affiliates"
description: "Enroll your event in the PLVR affiliate program and set a commission per ticket type."
---

Reward PLVR Affiliates for selling your tickets. Enroll the event once, then set a commission for each ticket type.

<Warning>
  Enrollment cannot be undone. Until you enroll, commission settings stay greyed out.
</Warning>

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/promotions/affiliates-enroll.png | Open Promotions > Affiliates before enrolling. Capture the Enroll button at the top and the greyed-out Commission Settings area. */}

## Add a commission

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enroll">Select **Enroll** (1).</Step>
  <Step title="Create">Select **Create** (2) below the Commission Settings table.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose the ticket">Tickets that already have a commission are not offered.</Step>
  <Step title="Set the commission">Choose **Percent (%)** or **Fixed Amount**, enter the amount, then select **Create**. A percentage is a whole number up to 100 (1 to 40% is recommended).</Step>
</Steps>

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/promotions/affiliates-commission-table.png | Affiliates page after enrolling with 3 ticket types configured (one Percent 10%, one Fixed $5, one Inactive). Capture the Enrolled button (1), the Create button (2) and the Commission Settings table. */}

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/promotions/affiliates-add-commission.png | Click Create below the Commission Settings table to open 'Add Commission'. Select a Ticket Type, Percent (%) and an amount of 10. Capture the panel including the ticket price under the Ticket Type field. */}

## Edit or pause

Use the menu at the end of a row and select **Edit** to change the type or amount. To pause a commission, set its **Commission Status** to **Inactive**.

<Tip>
  To track partners with your own links, create a [tracking link](/promotions/tracking-links) with an affiliate ID. Affiliate codes also appear in the [Ticket Report](/tickets/ticket-report).
</Tip>
