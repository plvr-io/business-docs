---
title: "Scenario: VIP Tickets and Add-ons"
description: "Offer a few premium tickets and sell extras such as parking alongside them."
---

**Goal:** Limited VIP tickets at a premium, plus extras buyers can add.

| Item | Quantity | Price | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| General Admission | 400 | $30 | Main ticket |
| VIP | 50 | $90 | Limited |
| Parking Pass (add-on) | 80 | $10 | General Admission and VIP |
| Event T-shirt (add-on) | 100 | $25 | VIP only |

## Part 1: Limit VIP

In [Ticket Management](/tickets/ticket-management), edit the VIP ticket:

- **Total Quantity** 50. When they are gone, VIP is sold out.
- **Limit Qty per Order** and **per User** (for example 4) to spread tickets across buyers.
- **Description** of what VIP includes.
- A later **Sales Start Date**, or keep it **Inactive**, to launch it as a second wave.

## Part 2: Create add-ons

On the **Add-ons** tab select **Create Add-on**, then:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Choose the **Session** and enter the **Add-on Name** and **Description**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who can buy it">
    **Linked to**: parking for General Admission and VIP, T-shirt for VIP only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the inventory">
    Use **Own inventory** for physical extras like parking and merchandise.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set quantity and price">
    **Total Quantity** and **Face Value**, then save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/tickets/vip-addons-create-addon.png | Create Add-on filled: Parking Pass, Active, Linked to General Admission and VIP, Own inventory, Quantity 80, Face Value 10. Callouts: 1 Linked to, 2 Inventory Source. */}

<Note>
  Add-ons cannot join a release chain, and their inventory source cannot be changed after creation.
</Note>

Sold add-ons appear in the [Ticket Report](/tickets/ticket-report) with an **Add-on** label.
