---
title: "Scenario: VIP Tickets and Add-ons"
description: "Offer a small number of premium tickets and sell extras such as parking or merchandise alongside them."
---

**Goal:** Sell a limited number of VIP tickets at a premium, and offer extras (parking, merchandise) that buyers can add.

## The example

| Item | Quantity | Price | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| General Admission | 400 | $30 | Main ticket |
| VIP | 50 | $90 | Limited |
| Parking Pass (add-on) | 80 | $10 | Linked to General Admission and VIP |
| Event T-shirt (add-on) | 100 | $25 | Linked to VIP only |

## Part 1: Limit your VIP tickets

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set a small Total Quantity">
    In [Ticket Management](/tickets/ticket-management), edit the VIP ticket and set **Total Quantity** to 50. Scarcity is built in: when 50 are sold, VIP is sold out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Limit how many one person can buy">
    Set **Limit Qty per Order** and **Limit Qty per User** (for example 4 and 4) so VIP tickets spread across more buyers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use a description">
    Add a **Description** (up to 200 characters) explaining what VIP includes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule when it opens">
    Choose a **Sales Start Date** later than General Admission if you want VIP to appear as a second wave, or keep it **Inactive** until you announce it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Part 2: Create add-ons

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Add-ons tab">
    In Ticket Management, select the **Add-ons** tab, then **Create Add-on**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and pick the session">
    Choose the **Session**, enter the **Add-on Name** (for example "Parking Pass"), set **Status**, and add a **Description** that explains it to attendees.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who can buy it">
    Under **Linked to**, select the tickets it can be purchased with. Parking: General Admission and VIP. T-shirt: VIP only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the inventory">
    **Own inventory** gives the add-on its own quantity (80 parking spaces). **Sync from ticket** uses another ticket type's inventory, so each add-on sold reduces that ticket's availability. Choose Own inventory for physical extras like parking and merchandise.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the price and limits">
    Enter **Total Quantity**, **Face Value**, and optional sales dates and per-order or per-user limits. Then save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Add-ons are not tickets: they cannot be added to a release chain, and the inventory source cannot be changed after creating them.
</Note>

## Track the results

The [Ticket Report](/tickets/ticket-report) lists add-ons with an **Add-on** label and shows an **Add-ons Sold** card when the event has add-ons.
