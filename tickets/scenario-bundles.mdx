**Goal:** Encourage groups to buy together with a discounted pack.

| Item | Includes | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General Admission | 1 ticket | $30 |
| Group of 4 | General Admission x 4 | $100 |

The buyer pays $100 instead of $120.

## How inventory works

A bundle draws from the ticket it includes. If General Admission has 300 tickets, one Group of 4 sale uses 4 of them. **Max Bundle Sales** caps the number of packs (for example 20), but a pack can only sell while enough of the included ticket remains.

## Manage a bundle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Edit Bundle">
    In [Ticket Management](/tickets/ticket-management), select the pencil on the bundle row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set price, cap and window">
    Set **Price**, **Max Bundle Sales**, sale dates and optional limits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate">
    Set **Status** to **Active** and **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/tickets/bundles-edit-panel.png | Edit Bundle panel for 'Group of 4'. Callouts: 1 Price, 2 Max Bundle Sales (20), 3 Included Tickets reading 'General Admission x 4', 4 Status. */}

<Warning>
  Name and **Price** lock after the first sale, and bundles cannot join a release chain.
</Warning>

- Bundle tickets show the bundle name in the [Ticket Report](/tickets/ticket-report).
- A [promo code](/promotions/promo-codes) can target one bundle.
- This portal cannot create new bundles. Ask your PLVR representative.
