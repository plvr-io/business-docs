---
title: "Scenario: Multi-day Festival"
description: "Run a festival with one session per day and track each day separately."
---

**Goal:** Friday, Saturday and Sunday, each sold and counted on its own.

| Session | Ticket | Quantity | Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Friday | Day Pass | 400 | $40 |
| Saturday | Day Pass | 600 | $55 |
| Sunday | Day Pass | 400 | $40 |

Each day is a **session** with its own capacity, prices and sale windows. Saturday selling out does not affect Friday.

<Note>
  Sessions and their tickets are set up with your event. For another day or ticket type, contact your PLVR representative.
</Note>

## Review each day

In [Ticket Management](/tickets/ticket-management), check each session's **Price**, **Total** and dates, set purchase limits for popular days, and keep a day **Inactive** until you announce it.

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/tickets/multi-day-ticket-management.png | Ticket Management for the three-session example. Callouts: 1 Session filter, 2 the three per-session tables. */}

## Tiers on every day

Build a [release chain](/tickets/scenario-ga-tiers) on one session, then:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy">
    Select **Copy to...** on the chain card and tick the other sessions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review matches">
    Tiers match by ticket name. Otherwise pick the right ticket, or **Don't copy this tier**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Auto-release">
    Copies start paused. Fix any flagged end times, then switch it on in each session.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/tickets/multi-day-copy-chain.png | Copy to... on the Friday chain with Saturday and Sunday ticked and tiers matched automatically. */}

<Tip>
  Use the same ticket names on every day so copying and reporting stay simple.
</Tip>

A single pass valid for all days is not something you build in this portal. Talk to your PLVR representative.

## Track and operate

- [Ticket Report](/tickets/ticket-report): filter by **Session** for one day's sales.
- [Comp tickets](/tickets/scenario-comp-guests): choose the **Session** so guests get the right day.
- [Promo codes](/tickets/scenario-promo-discounts): target one ticket in one session to discount a slow day.
