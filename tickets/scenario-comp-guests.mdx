---
title: "Scenario: Comping Guests, Artists, and Press"
description: "Issue free tickets to artists, press, sponsors, and staff, and understand how they affect your capacity."
---

**Goal:** Give free entry to people who should not pay, while keeping count of your capacity.

## What to expect

- Comp tickets are real tickets of a chosen **Ticket Type** in a chosen **Session**.
- They come out of the same inventory as paid tickets. If General Admission has 300 tickets and you comp 20, 280 remain for sale.
- Ticket types that are sold out appear greyed out in the comp form, so you cannot comp them.

<Warning>
  Comps count against the ticket you choose. Decide before you sell out whether to reserve inventory for guests, or create a dedicated ticket type for them (ask your PLVR representative if you need one) so paid sales are unaffected.
</Warning>

## Example: 30 press and artist guests

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare your list">
    Collect each guest's name, email, and how many tickets they need. Use a spreadsheet with columns Name, Email, Quantity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Complimentary Tickets">
    Go to [Complimentary Tickets](/tickets/comp-tickets) and select **Create Complimentary Tickets**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Bulk Creation">
    Use **Bulk Creation** for up to 50 recipients in one go. Pick the **Session** and **Ticket Type** (everyone receives the same type).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import or enter recipients">
    Paste or upload your CSV, then review the rows. Fix any line the portal flags.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide on email">
    Turn on **Email tickets to recipients** to send tickets directly, or leave it off and download each person's tickets later. Emailing allows up to 10 tickets per recipient.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add remarks and save">
    A remark such as "Press" or "Artist guest list" helps you find them later. Select **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For one or two guests, use **Single Creation** instead.

## Different types of guests

If artists and press need different ticket types (for example VIP for artists, General Admission for press), run one bulk batch for each ticket type.

## After sending

- The comp table shows **Total Recipients** and **Total Tickets**, and each row's **Sent Date**.
- Use the download icon on a row to get a recipient's tickets as PDFs.
- Comp tickets show up in your event's ticket counts, so remember to include them when you plan capacity.
