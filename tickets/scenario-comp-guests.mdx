---
title: "Scenario: Comping Guests, Artists, and Press"
description: "Issue free tickets to artists, press, sponsors and staff while tracking capacity."
---

**Goal:** Free entry for people who should not pay, without losing count of capacity.

Comps come out of the same inventory as paid tickets: comp 20 of 300 General Admission and 280 remain for sale.

<Warning>
  Decide before you sell out whether to reserve inventory for guests. If you need a dedicated comp ticket type, ask your PLVR representative.
</Warning>

## Example: 30 press and artist guests

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare the list">
    A spreadsheet with Name, Email, Quantity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Bulk Creation">
    In [Complimentary Tickets](/tickets/comp-tickets), select **Create Complimentary Tickets** then **Bulk Creation**. Pick the **Session** and **Ticket Type**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import and review">
    Paste or upload the CSV and fix any flagged line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Email or download">
    Turn on email to send directly (max 10 per recipient), or leave it off and download PDFs later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a remark and save">
    A remark such as "Press" makes the batch easy to find.
  </Step>
</Steps>

- One batch per ticket type: if artists get VIP and press gets General Admission, run two batches.
- For one or two guests, use **Single Creation**.
