---
title: "Scenario: General Admission in Tiers"
description: "Sell General Admission in stages and let a release chain open each tier automatically."
---

**Goal:** One kind of entry, with the price rising as demand grows.

| Tier | Quantity | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Early Bird | 100 | $20 |
| General Admission | 300 | $30 |
| Final Release | 100 | $40 |

Capacity 500. Revenue if everything sells: 100 x $20 + 300 x $30 + 100 x $40 = $15,000.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Use a release chain when">
    You want the next price to open the moment the previous tier is gone, even overnight.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Use sale dates only when">
    You want to switch tiers on fixed dates, or by hand. You can also combine both: dates as a safety net, the chain to advance early.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/tickets/ga-tiers-initial-tickets.png | Ticket Management with the three tickets before any chain exists: Early Bird Active, the other two Inactive, each with a future sales end date. */}

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the tickets">
    Confirm **Price**, **Total** and dates in [Ticket Management](/tickets/ticket-management). Name and price lock after the first sale.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set statuses and end times">
    Early Bird **Active**, the other two **Inactive**. Every tier needs a future end time, each later tier ending no earlier than the one before.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the chain">
    In **Release Chains**, select **New chain**, name it "GA" and **Create**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add tiers in order">
    **+ Add existing ticket**: Early Bird, General Admission, Final Release.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Auto-release">
    The chain starts paused until you do.
  </Step>
</Steps>

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/tickets/ga-tiers-chain-configured.png | Release Chains panel after setup: chain 'GA' with the three tiers in order, Auto-release ON, Early Bird 'On sale' with a partly filled bar. Callouts: 1 Auto-release, 2 the '98%' line, 3 Activity log icon. */}

## As tickets sell

Buyers see only the open tier. When it is nearly sold out (at the percentage shown in the panel, not necessarily the last ticket), the next tier turns **Active** by itself. After Final Release the chain shows **Exhausted**.

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/tickets/ga-tiers-second-tier-open.png | After Early Bird has sold out: Release Chains panel and ticket table together. Early Bird 'Sold out - next tier opened', General Admission Active at $30 with the auto-activated note and 'GA - 2/3' badge. */}

<Note>
  Unpaid orders in checkout can briefly hold tickets. If released, an earlier tier may show a few tickets back on sale.
</Note>

<Tip>
  There is no tier list or countdown on the buyer page. Explain the staging in each ticket's **Description**, for example "Early Bird: first 100 tickets only".
</Tip>

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/tickets/ga-tiers-attendee-view.png | Public event page (via 'view Event') with only Early Bird Active: one General Admission option and the Description 'Early Bird: first 100 tickets only'. */}

## Keep an eye on it

- **Activity log** (history icon) records every sell-out and activation.
- Switch **Auto-release** off to pause. Tickets keep their status.
- If you activate a later tier by hand, the earlier ones will not auto-activate. Activate or remove them.

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/tickets/ga-tiers-activity-log.png | Activity log open with a few entries (auto-release on, sell-out, automatic activation). */}

## Reuse for other sessions

Use **Copy to...** on the chain card. Tiers are matched by name, and copies start paused, so turn on **Auto-release** after reviewing. See [Multi-day festival](/tickets/scenario-multi-day-festival).
