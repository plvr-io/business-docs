This page is for whoever brings people to your event. All of the tools below sit under **Promotions** in an event's side menu, plus **RSVP** and **Followers**.

<Note>
  The organization-level **Marketing** section is currently a placeholder with no tools. Use the event-level pages below. See [Marketing](/other/marketing).
</Note>

## What you can do

- Create discount codes with limits and time windows, and see how each performs.
- Build trackable links and QR codes for each channel and see their clicks.
- Enroll your event in the affiliate program and set a commission per ticket type.
- Email ticket holders, interested followers or your own list, and track opens, clicks and revenue.
- See who follows your event and review RSVP requests.
- Understand who your attendees are, and how many return.

## Recommended path

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plan your promotion">
    Read the [promote your event guide](/guides/promote-your-event).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a tracking link per channel">
    One link for each place you post, so you can see what works. See [Tracking links](/promotions/tracking-links).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add promo codes">
    For early birds, partners or special groups. See [Promo codes](/promotions/promo-codes).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring in affiliates">
    Set commissions in [Affiliates](/promotions/affiliates).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send campaigns">
    Email your audience with [Email campaigns](/promotions/campaigns).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review results">
    Compare codes, links and campaigns, then check [Audience](/organization/audience) for who came.
  </Step>
</Steps>
