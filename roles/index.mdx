## What each organization role can access

Every team member has one of these roles in your organization. The owner is the person who created it. See [Team and roles](/organization/team) for how to add people and change roles.

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/roles/roles-header-as-scanner-vs-owner.png | Two header crops stacked, labelled: the portal header as an Owner (Events, Table Booking, Audience, Team, Organization all visible) and as a Manager (no Team link). Crop each to the header bar only. */}

| Header link | Owner | Admin | Manager | Scanner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Events** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Audience** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Table Booking** | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| **Organization** | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| **Team** | Yes | Yes | No | No |

If you open a page your role does not allow, you see **Access denied**. Scanners are intended for ticket scanning in PLVR Scan, so most scanner work happens outside the Business Portal.

<Note>
  Roles in the table are organization roles. People who work at the event itself, such as gate, wristband and top-up teams, sign in with a login code instead. See [Onsite staff](/onsite/onsite-staff) and the [door and onsite staff guide](/roles/door-and-onsite-staff).
</Note>
