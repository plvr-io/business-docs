The core team sets up the organization, builds each event and takes it live. Owners and admins can open **Team**. Managers have everything else, including **Organization**, **Table Booking** and all event pages.

| Page | Use it to |

## Recommended path

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your organization and team">
    [Organization settings](/organization/settings), then [Team and roles](/organization/team).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Understand tickets">
    Read [Ticket concepts](/tickets/concepts), with examples in [General admission tiers](/tickets/scenario-ga-tiers) and [Multi-day festival](/tickets/scenario-multi-day-festival).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the event">
    [Event editor](/events/event-editor), [Ticket management](/tickets/ticket-management), [Registration form](/events/registration-form).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live and promote">
    Follow the [launch checklist](/guides/launch-checklist) and [promote your event](/guides/promote-your-event). Hand promotion to the [Marketing team](/roles/marketing-team).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare event day">
    Create staff codes and follow the [event day runbook](/guides/event-day-runbook).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wrap up">
    Use [After the event](/guides/after-the-event).
  </Step>
</Steps>

New to the portal? Start with the [Quickstart](/getting-started/quickstart).

## Good habits

- Give scanners only **Scanner** and reserve **Admin** for people who manage the team.
- Remove people when they leave.
- If you change your own role to one that cannot open Team, you may lose access straight away.
