For whoever brings people to your event. The tools sit under **Promotions** in an event's side menu, plus **RSVP** and **Followers**.

<Note>
  The organization-level **Marketing** section is a placeholder with no tools. Use the event-level pages. See [Marketing](/other/marketing).
</Note>

{/* SCREENSHOT: images/roles/marketing-promotions-menu.png | Inside an event: the left side menu with Promotions expanded showing Promo Codes, Campaigns, Door Sales, Tracking Links and Affiliates, with Followers and RSVP also visible. Crop to the side menu. */}

| Page | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| [Promo codes](/promotions/promo-codes) | Discounts and per-code results. |
| [Tracking links](/promotions/tracking-links) | See which channels bring people in. |
| [Affiliates](/promotions/affiliates) | Pay commission on partner sales. |
| [Email campaigns](/promotions/campaigns) | Reach ticket holders, followers or your own list. |
| [Door sales](/promotions/door-sales) | QR code and poster for onsite sales. |
| [Followers](/events/followers) | See who follows the event. |
| [RSVP](/events/rsvp) | Review and approve requests. |
| [Audience](/organization/audience) | Attendee insights across events. |

## Recommended path

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plan">
    Read [Promote your event](/guides/promote-your-event).
  </Step>
  <Step title="One tracking link per channel">
    See [Tracking links](/promotions/tracking-links).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add promo codes">
    See [Promo codes](/promotions/promo-codes).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring in affiliates">
    Set commissions in [Affiliates](/promotions/affiliates).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send campaigns">
    See [Email campaigns](/promotions/campaigns).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review results">
    Compare codes, links and campaigns, then check [Audience](/organization/audience).
  </Step>
</Steps>
