Managers have access to PLVR Business and PLVR Scan, except Team Management. They can open **Organization** and **Table Booking**, and everything inside an event. This page is for the person who builds and runs an event.

## What you can do

- Edit the public event page: images, name, times, description and terms.
- Set ticket prices, quantities and sale windows, and create add-ons.
- Issue complimentary tickets.
- Choose the questions buyers answer at checkout.
- Change an event's visibility and watch its sales.
- Create login codes for your onsite team.
- Review RSVP requests and see who follows the event.
