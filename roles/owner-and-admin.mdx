Owners and admins have full access to PLVR Business and PLVR Scan. They are the only roles that can open **Team**. This page is for the people who set the organization up and keep it running.

## What you can do

- Edit your public organizer page: logo, name, description, links and more.
- Add team members, change their role, and remove them.
- Set up table booking for your venue.
- See every event, change its visibility, and read its sales overview.
- Review audience insights across your events.
- Do everything a manager can do inside an event.

## Recommended path

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your organization">
    Fill in your profile on **Organization**. See [Organization settings](/organization/settings).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Open **Team**, select **Add**, and give each person the smallest role that lets them do their job. See [Team and roles](/organization/team).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your first event live">
    Follow the [Quickstart](/getting-started/quickstart) or [Onboarding](/getting-started/onboarding), then work through the [launch checklist](/guides/launch-checklist).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Oversee as it runs">
    Check [Events overview](/events/overview) for sales, and use the [event day runbook](/guides/event-day-runbook) to prepare your team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review afterwards">
    Use the [after the event guide](/guides/after-the-event) to close out reports and learn from the numbers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good habits

- Give scanners only the Scanner role, and managers the Manager role. Reserve Admin for people who need to manage the team.
- Remove people from the team when they leave.
- If you change your own role to one that cannot open Team, you may lose access to it straight away.
