This page is for whoever reconciles money and reports on how the event went. Most reports can be exported, so you can take the numbers into your own tools.

## What each report answers

| Question | Where to look |
| --- | --- |
| How is the event selling overall? | [Events overview](/events/overview) |
| Who bought which ticket or add-on? | [Ticket report](/tickets/ticket-report) |
| How did each promo code perform? | [Promo codes](/promotions/promo-codes) |
| Which channels and campaigns brought revenue? | [Tracking links](/promotions/tracking-links) and [Email campaigns](/promotions/campaigns) |
| How much did each vendor and item sell, and what do we owe vendors? | [Spending report](/tap/spending-report) |
| Every top-up and spend, one line at a time? | [Cashless activity](/tap/cashless-activity) |
| Is a top-up unresolved at the desk? | [Cashless (Onsite Operations)](/onsite/cashless) |
| Who paid a custom amount? | [Payment links](/other/payment-links) |
| Who comes back to my events? | [Audience](/organization/audience) |

The cashless pages only appear for events that use PLVR Tap. See [PLVR Tap overview](/tap/introduction).

## Recommended path

<Steps>
  <Step title="During sales">
    Watch [Events overview](/events/overview) and export the [Ticket report](/tickets/ticket-report) when you need a list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="On event day">
    Use [Cashless activity](/tap/cashless-activity) to follow top-ups and spending, and [Cashless](/onsite/cashless) to resolve unresolved top-ups.
  </Step>
  <Step title="After the event">
    Export the summaries and vendor settlement reports from the [Spending report](/tap/spending-report), and review payment links you created.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close out">
    Follow the [after the event guide](/guides/after-the-event).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who can see reports

Managers, admins and owners can open the event pages above. See [Guides by role](/roles) for what each organization role can access.
